# 📌 Project Overview

This project was undertaken as part of the **Business Data Management (BDM) Capstone Project** offered under the **IIT Madras BS Degree Programme**. The objective was to conduct an end-to-end business analysis of a real-world cement dealership by understanding its operational workflow, identifying key business challenges, analysing historical business data, and developing practical, data-driven strategies to improve overall business performance.

The project followed the complete business analytics lifecycle, beginning with understanding the organisation's operations and identifying the major issues affecting its growth. Initial discussions with the business owner were conducted to understand existing workflows, operational processes, financial practices, and day-to-day challenges. Based on these discussions, the primary business problems were identified, including high transportation costs, delayed customer payments, environmental concerns arising from cement handling, labour shortages, and low profitability caused by intense market competition.

Since no structured digital records were maintained by the business, primary data was collected manually through owner discussions and existing business records. The collected information was organised into a structured Microsoft Excel dataset covering an **8-month period (August 2024 – March 2025)**. Data preprocessing involved organising monthly operational and financial information, validating entries, creating Pivot Tables, and preparing the dataset for further statistical and business analysis.

Multiple business analytics techniques were applied throughout the project. **Descriptive Analysis** was performed to understand business performance using summary statistics such as mean, median, mode, minimum, maximum, and standard deviation. **Diagnostic Analysis** was used to investigate the root causes behind operational inefficiencies, inconsistent profitability, and cost variations. **Trend Analysis** was carried out to identify seasonal demand patterns, monthly sales fluctuations, inventory trends, and business performance over time. Various charts and visualisations were developed in Microsoft Excel to support the interpretation of business data and communicate insights effectively.

Based on the analytical findings, the project proposed practical business recommendations to improve operational efficiency and long-term sustainability. These recommendations included transportation cost optimisation through electric vehicle adoption, strategies to reduce credit and payment risks using digital invoicing and customer segmentation, improved inventory planning based on seasonal demand trends, operational improvements to address labour shortages, and environmentally sustainable practices to minimise the impact of cement dust.

Overall, this project demonstrates the complete application of business analytics—from problem identification and primary data collection to data preparation, statistical analysis, business interpretation, and strategic decision-making—to solve real-world business challenges using data-driven methodologies.
